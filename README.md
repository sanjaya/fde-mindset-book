# The FDE Mindset

*From Shipping Code to Delivering Intelligence*

By **Sanjaya Kumar Panigrahy**

A short book on how a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) thinks. Strong AI models are now available to everyone, so the model is no longer what separates a system that works from one that fails. What separates them is everything built around the model, and the judgement of the person who builds it.

## Who it is for

Engineers, architects and engineering managers moving from building software to deploying AI for customers, and anyone preparing for a Forward Deployed Engineer role.

## Ideas in one line each

- The agent is the wrapper. Intelligence is the product.
- The model is about twenty percent of a working system. The rest is yours to build.
- The first statement of a problem is almost always the ordinary one.
- A model's score is a ceiling on the system, never a forecast.
- Trust is a function of accuracy and consistency.
- You are responsible for what your AI tells your customers.

## Source and use

The book is compiled from study material for a programme on Forward Deployed AI Engineering, rewritten as a continuous read. It is shared for personal learning.
